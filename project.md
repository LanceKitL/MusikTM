# Musikk™ - Worship Team Sync.

---

Material UI (Android)

> Believe that you can do everything, you’ll learn by doing it.
> 

This application eases the pain of Music Directors and Team Members, providing them an all in one tool for managing line-ups, schedule, and promotes communication.

**Problem to solve**

- Music Team management is hard if you cannot track your member availability.
- When team has a cifra, we oftens go back and fort to youtube tutorials to learn, but we believe ear training and being able to keep up with the team matters more.
- We’re also going to bridge the communication gap, most of the music team here in the Philippines uses *Facebook Messenger* as the main communication tool, but when you have a lot of work outside church you often forget to check your **Group Chat**.
- Our current church praise team relies heavily on song leaders hand signal, while this is actually nice to practice

**Features**

- Schedule Management
    - Music Directors can easily assign Team Members, no need to go back to tradition Excel File that does nothing to remind them.
    - Team Members can self tag availability status pushing notifications that will remind everyone instantly.
- Cifra
    - When song leader sends the line up, lyrics and chords automatically shows up on Team Members.
    - When song leader has custom changes on the key and arrangement, it should sync properly to team members, so on the spot requests shouldn’t be a problem anymore.
    - Music Team members can remove instrument of their choice, and jam with their instrument.
- Praisent
    - Aesthetic **Lyrics Presenter** utilizing web.

**System Flow**

1. User Creates an account
    1. Musikk Director
        1. (GCLASS-ROOM concept) generates a unique code.
        2. Dashboard + Onboarding
        3. Creating a schedule (also sends a reminder to the team)
            1. consists of Musikk Team members with their roles.
        4. Announcement Section (where music director can say whatever they want to say) 
    2. Musikk Team 
        1. They pick their role (Drummer, Bassist, Guitarist, Song Lead, Back up, etc)
        2. They join a Team (with team code)
        3. They get their dashboard + onboarding
